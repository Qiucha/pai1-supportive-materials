# Glossary

**Lab** — An interactive panel in a lesson where the learner changes inputs with controls and watches the results. A figure with no controls is not a lab.

**Guide** — A short scripted walk through one lab that teaches 1–3 core ideas. It opens the first time the lab is fully on screen, can be skipped or replayed, and ends in explore freely.

**Step** — One unit of a guide. It asks for one action on a real control and ends with a notice. A step may start with a prediction.

**Prediction** — The learner's explicit choice about what will happen, made before acting. It is shown next to the actual result.

**Setup** — A condition the lab must meet before a step's action makes sense. The guide points to it but never does it for the learner.

**Notice** — The line that replaces a step's instruction once the step is done. It says what changed and where to look.

**Explore freely** — The state of a lab after its guide is finished or skipped. All controls belong to the learner, and an open question is shown.

**Bridge** — The link between two separate labs in the same week: a "Next lab" link shown in explore freely, and a first step in the next guide that builds on what the previous lab showed.
